We tracked every early-stage fund that closed a new vehicle between 1 January and 29 September 2026 in four industries: AI, deeptech, fintech and B2B software. 89 funds made the list. Here are the top 20, and the full list to download.

## 1. Why a newly closed fund is your best first call

A venture fund typically has three to four years to put its new capital into first cheques, then spends the rest of its life on follow-ons. Right after a close, the partners are measured on one thing: finding the companies that will define the fund.

That changes the conversation. A new fund is actively looking, open to new relationships, and more willing to lead. A fund in year four of its cycle is mostly reserving capital for the companies it already owns.

## 4. How to use the list before you send anything

1. **Filter on stage first, industry second.** A fund that writes $5M first cheques is not your pre-seed lead, however good the thesis fit.
2. **Read the last five deals.** Recent portfolio companies tell you what the partners actually buy.
3. **Check where the fund is in its cycle.** A first close is still raising. A final close in the last 12 months is deploying hardest.
4. **Lead with proof, not a deck.** One line on what you do, one line of traction, and a link to your data room.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which VC funds closed new funds in 2026?**
89 early-stage funds in AI, deeptech, fintech and B2B software closed new vehicles between January and September 2026. The largest early-stage closes include Greylock 18 ($1.5B), Lux Ventures IX ($1.5B), Primary Venture Partners Fund V ($625M) and South Park Commons Fund IV ($575M).

**Why should founders pitch a newly closed fund?**
A new fund has fresh capital and a set number of first cheques to write before its next raise. The partners are actively looking for the companies that will define the fund, which makes them more open to new relationships and more willing to lead.

**What is the difference between a first close and a final close?**
A first close means the fund has enough commitments to start investing but is still raising. A final close means fundraising is finished. Funds at first close often deploy quickly to show early results to the investors they are still courting.

**How do I find the right fund for my startup on this list?**
Filter by stage and first cheque size first, then by industry. Then read each fund's recent deals to confirm its real focus before you reach out.
